01
INTRODUCTION

I explore how
matter responds
to light.

I am an experimental physicist exploring matter through terahertz spectroscopy, ultrafast light–matter interactions and magnetism.

I am currently working as a Project Associate in the group of Prof. Shriganesh Prabhu at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Mumbai.

My research is driven by a fascination with understanding how matter responds to electromagnetic excitation on ultrafast timescales. I am particularly interested in magnetic and functional materials and in exploring their dynamics through spectroscopic techniques.

Beyond the laboratory, I am passionate about science communication and education. I enjoy developing hands-on science activities, DIY experiments and interactive demonstrations that encourage students to explore scientific ideas through curiosity and experimentation.
